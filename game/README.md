# My Talking Tom 2 — Fan Game 🐱

A little virtual-pet game inspired by Outfit7's My Talking Tom 2, all in one file.

## How to play

Just open `talking-tom.html` in any browser (phone or computer).

- 🛋️ **Play** — tap Tom to pet and tickle him, tell him jokes to make him happy
- 🍗 **Food** — feed him snacks when he's hungry (eating makes him need the potty!)
- 🛁 **Bath** — take him to the potty when he has to pee/poop, and when he's dirty
  scrub him with **color soaps** (even rainbow soap 🌈). If you ignore the potty
  bar... he has an accident 💩
- 🛏️ **Sleep** — turn the light off and he falls asleep to get his energy back
- ✈️ **Fly** — airplane mini-game! Tap to fly, grab coins 🪙, dodge storm clouds ⛈️.
  Coins become tickets 🎟️ you spend in the **Beast Prize Shop** to unlock
  MrBeast prizes (Beast Cap Tom can wear, Feastables chocolate, Beast Burger,
  money pile, private island, golden play button).

Your Tom is saved automatically, so he remembers you when you come back —
but his needs keep going while you're away, so take good care of him!
